Weaken II: Causal Alternatives, Plans & Weaken-EXCEPT

🕑 ~75 min · Archetype A — LR Deep Dive

Weaken II: Causal Alternatives, Plans & Weaken-EXCEPT

Rounding out Weaken with plan arguments and the EXCEPT variant, mirroring yesterday's Strengthen counterpart.

1. Weakening a Plan

Show the plan won't be implemented as described, won't actually produce the predicted effect, or will trigger a side effect that undermines the goal.

2. Weaken-EXCEPT

Four choices weaken; the correct one is neutral or actually strengthens. Reread the stem before touching the choices.

Practice items on this page are curriculum-authored in LSAT style; they are not official LSAC questions. Real drilling happens on LawHub — see the assignment below.

Q1. Argument: "Installing solar panels will cut the factory's energy costs in half." Which of the following would NOT weaken this claim?

A. The factory's roof cannot support the required panel weight without costly reinforcement
B. Local energy prices have historically been volatile, unrelated to the factory's own equipment
C. The factory operates mostly at night, when solar panels generate no power
D. Panel maintenance costs are projected to offset most of the savings
Correct answer: B. Historical price volatility unrelated to the factory's own situation doesn't bear on whether solar panels will cut its costs — it's the answer that fails to weaken.
